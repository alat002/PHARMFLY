ABOUT PHARMFLY

Medicine When It Matters

PHARMFLY is a prototype emergency medicine delivery platform designed to connect users with nearby pharmacies and provide rapid delivery options through drones and vans.

The platform helps users search for medicines, identify nearby pharmacies, request delivery and track their order.
